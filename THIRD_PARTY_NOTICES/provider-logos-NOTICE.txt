Source
  The 128x128 PNG marks were copied from the DNS Migrator project
  (apps/web/public/providers). DNS Migrator rendered them from:
    - Simple Icons glyphs with brand fills (CC0 1.0, https://simpleicons.org):
      Cloudflare, Akamai, GoDaddy, Hetzner, Namecheap.
    - The official AWS artwork export: Amazon Web Services (route53, route53-dark).
    - Vendor-published raster marks: Google Cloud (gcp), Microsoft Azure (azure),
      IBM NS1 Connect (ns1, ns1-dark).

Trademarks
  Cloudflare, Akamai, Amazon Web Services, AWS, Route 53, GoDaddy, Namecheap,
  Hetzner, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, IBM and NS1 are trademarks of their
  respective owners. AstraNull is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed
  by any of them.

  The marks are used only to identify the third-party service a connector
  reads from or a manual metadata record describes (nominative use). They are
  shown unmodified, at small size, next to the provider's name, and never as
  AstraNull branding.

  Remove or replace a mark if its owner's brand guidelines require it.
